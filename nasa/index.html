NASA Vulnerability Disclosure Program

NASA runs a public vulnerability disclosure program through Bugcrowd. Anyone may test the agency's internet-facing assets within the published scope and report what they find. There is no bounty. The program pays in recognition, and only for findings that NASA's own security team validates and fixes.

I reported two vulnerabilities. Both were validated, accepted and remediated: one rated P1 critical, one rated P2 high. Each produced its own letter of recognition, and the pair placed me in the program's Hall of Fame.

Verifying this independently

My public Bugcrowd profile lists NASA under Hall of Fame. That is a third-party record, maintained by the platform, not by me.

NASA also publishes the full Hall of Fame roster, though it is paginated and the ordering shifts as researchers are added, so the profile link above is the faster check.
