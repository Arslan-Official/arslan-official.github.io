# Published, and deliberately left off

## Published

- HTB CPTS certificate — the strongest credential on the page
- Synack Red Team onboarding certificate — labelled as onboarding, not engagement work
- CNSP (The SecOps Group) and Google Cybersecurity — both carry issuer verification links
- PSO certificate of internship
- NASA letters of recognition, P1 and P2, reproduced in full
- NASA Hall of Fame listing and both Crowdstream entries

The NASA letters carry no report identifiers, so nothing needed redacting.

## Left off, deliberately

- 6 × Udemy ZSecurity certificates, Udemy After Effects, Google AI Essentials
- Forage Mastercard job simulation — built for candidates with no real experience
- The old project GIFs: keylogger, reverse backdoor, phishing simulator,
  ARP spoofer, network scanner — course exercises that read as junior next to
  a CVSS 9.0 RCE
- Any Synack engagement material — under NDA

## One thing to watch

The Synack certificate is a course-completion document from onboarding. The site
says exactly that. Do not let it get re-captioned as proof of engagement work:
if one claim on the page is overstated, a reviewer will discount the rest.
